# SHEETING LEXUS HSM-CTM7 — whole-machine audit

Asset BMJ-MCH-0002, audited as one complete right-to-left production line: hydraulic unwind, web carrier and tension rollers, draw/cut zone, high-speed take-away, slow overlap/shingling, stack/lay table, controls and operator access.

## Evidence and dimensional boundary

Primary installed evidence is the nine-photo BMJ set IMG_2479–IMG_2487 dated 22 September 2026. It verifies the single loaded reel with paired support arms, deep threaded web path, asymmetric LEXUS cabinet, exterior draw roll, open belt delivery, manual rack-adjusted stack table, console and catwalk. HSM 56 family literature corroborates only the “Flat Bed Knife” wording and generic hidden process relationships. No public HSM-CTM7 engineering drawing was found; exact internal knife, drive, tension calibration, speed ratios and installed option set remain `BLOCKED_EVIDENCE`.

## Audit results and repairs

- **Unwind/input:** one reel, left/right arm pair, chucks, hydraulic supports and hose references remain photo-oriented and clear the low entry roller. The previously removed fictional longitudinal second station remains absent.
- **Web carrier/tension:** the continuous web follows the low entry roll and seven alternating guide/tension rollers without entering their cores. Roller rotation signs continue to follow the represented contact side.
- **Draw/cutter:** the black draw roll and guarded cut mouth remain mounted. Material separation is visible in simulation, but no blade/revolver geometry is invented. Taxonomy text was corrected because it incorrectly claimed that an internal mechanism appeared in cutaway.
- **Take-away/overlap:** detached sheets begin at web velocity, accelerate without teleporting, decelerate onto slower tapes and form physical shingling before stack entry. The following material remains a continuous attached ribbon until its cut.
- **Stack/output:** sheets stay face-up, enter above the actual pile top, settle on the lift/pallet support and accumulate. Manual guide handwheels and screws remain stationary; only the evidence-bounded lift compensation moves.
- **Control:** the HMI bezel was a solid plate covering the display. It is now a four-piece open frame aligned with the sloped console. The emergency-stop collar was a solid disc around the wrong control; it is now an open ring concentric with the red control.
- **Interior/LOD:** the fixed console was incorrectly treated as a removable cover, leaving its display and buttons floating in interior mode. The console now remains mounted. Cutter door seams, handles and hinges leave with their exterior panels, and HMI identity remains visible at low detail.
- **Access/mounting:** existing catwalk, steps, rails, machine feet and stack anchors remain grounded; photo-verified machine dimensions, orientation and process direction were not changed.
