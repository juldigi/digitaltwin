## Dimensional and evidence limits

No changes to dimensions-offset5.js, installed module positions, pitch, scale or simulation anchors. No new process equipment or replacement guessed pan. New liner and inspection shape details are photo-derived proportions; exact hidden geometry and the whole multi-angle silhouette are not claimed to be accepted. The available browser cannot render WebGL.

The fountain-specific taxonomy now distinguishes a photo-observed inclined liner from unmeasured thickness/slope. The complete machine is still a reconstruction; CAD-level fidelity of hidden frames, journal recesses, hoses and sensor mountings requires additional direct evidence. CI checks establish structural consistency and lifecycle behavior, not visual identity to the actual machine.
