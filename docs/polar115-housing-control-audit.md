# POLAR 115 EM-MON — housing and control audit

Asset BMJ-MCH-0001 / GLM-1 / serial 5831536. Scope: base and cutter-head housing, side-drive enclosure, EM-MON display, keypad, mode/pressure controls, normal/interior/LOD presentation and taxonomy.

Evidence: installed BMJ photographs IMG_2488.jpeg–IMG_2491.jpeg verify the rectangular EM-era head, cabinet, side-drive inspection window, small square display, physical keypad/controls and taped instruction sheets. The registered archive is used only for family architecture; no modern touchscreen or unobserved control hardware is inferred.

## Findings and repairs

- The refinement pass represented the display bezel and keypad surround as solid cuboids. They physically covered the screen and key field from the operator side. Each is now a four-piece open frame, retaining the evidenced legacy silhouette without occluding the controls.
- The console face and key-switch plate were incorrectly classified as removable exterior covers. Interior/simulation mode consequently detached the complete operator interface. These fixed interfaces now remain mounted while only explicitly tagged service covers open.
- The cutter head, rear throat, cabinet, side-drive enclosure, inspection window and floor interfaces retain their previously verified geometry and side orientation. No housing dimensions or installed hardware were added.
- Tests now reject a bezel over the screen center, a surround over any key center, and loss of the display/keypad in interior or low-detail presentation.

## Evidence boundary

Exact cabinet panel construction, fastener pattern, electrical layout behind the console, switch labels and serial-specific control wiring are `BLOCKED_EVIDENCE`. They remain unmodeled rather than guessed.
