## Checks and limits

Tests sample almost two seconds of running simulation, verify small contact clearance, positive lift and forwarding, pause/stop behavior, constant pile top, moving supports, no paper intersection by blast outlets or rear feet, and removal of unverified rake solids. Existing photo, geometry, taxonomy and complete Offset 5 reality tests also run.

Cup count, hidden preset drive locations, pneumatic circuit, installed rake configuration, exact portal fabrication dimensions, sheet stock and suction cam phase remain unverified. Photo proportions do not become installation CAD. The transition is a functional process illustration; exact pickup-sheet deformation and phase locking are not asserted.
