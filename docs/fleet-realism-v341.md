# V341 fleet realism review

The review covers all 41 registry identities and photo-derived IPAL equipment. Passing computational checks does not certify serial-specific CAD, dimensions, installed options, or photorealistic visual fidelity. Actual BMJ photos/configuration override family literature. Unknown models and options remain unknown.

## Corrections

- Seven compressor models no longer carry an invented factory ring main / receiver / post-treatment network in their normal equipment envelope. The functional system diagram remains available for interior study through the same taxonomy.
- Five neutral AHUs and SANSIN AHU7 keep unverified external duct/interconnect schematics out of their normal equipment silhouette. Core indoor/outdoor packages remain distinct.
- QF-100CS retains its hydraulic unit at low LOD. X/Y taxonomy now targets the actual drive geometry.
- Diana Eye55 operator terminal taxonomy targets its physical pedestal. Both inspection recipes are software nodes, without fabricated mechanical bearing/fastener descendants.
- Active photo-derived IPAL has continuous curved stair stringers/handrails, shaft-aligned pump flanges, coupling and mounting feet. The separate utility-source builder also corrects inverted hopper cones, unsupported vessel legs and rotating motor housing.
- Fleet silhouette checks now use rendered meshes with inherited visibility. Hidden objects can no longer make a collapsed normal model pass. Every taxonomy mesh reference is checked, including partially resolved nodes.
- Offset5 V340 normal exterior, automatic simulation interior, open upper deck, locked junctions and compact mobile card are preserved.
